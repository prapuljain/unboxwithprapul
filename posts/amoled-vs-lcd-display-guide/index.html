AMOLED vs LCD: Which Display Should You Choose?

A simple explanation of AMOLED and LCD displays, with practical advice for phone, tablet and laptop buyers.

The display affects your experience every day

The screen is the part of a gadget you look at the most. That is why display quality matters more than many people think.

AMOLED advantages

AMOLED screens usually offer deeper blacks, higher contrast and more attractive colors. They are great for movies, social media and always-on display features.

LCD advantages

LCD screens can still be good, especially in budget devices. A high-quality LCD can look better than a poor-quality AMOLED panel.

What should you choose?

Choose AMOLED if you watch a lot of videos, want punchy colors and like premium-looking screens. Choose a good LCD if your budget is tight and the device gives better processor, battery or software support.

Prapul's take

Do not choose only by display type. Check brightness, refresh rate, touch response and reviews before deciding.
